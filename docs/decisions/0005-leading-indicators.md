# 5. Leading indicators replace days without incident

Status: accepted, November 2026

## Context

The office board said days without incident while eight risk factors were present on one battery job.

## Decision

Measure the conditions before incidents: risk factors per job, red and yellow jobs, near misses and good catches. Days since the last incident is still shown, labelled as a lagging number.

## Consequences

- Action happens before the job, while it can still prevent an incident.
- More numbers to explain. Each one says what it is for.

## Considered

- Incident rates only: simple, but they say nothing about tomorrow.
