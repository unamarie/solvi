Quality Gate

A small game about data quality, in the spirit of the 90s games I grew up with. Catch the good records, let the junk sink. Every bad record you let pass costs the company's trust in its numbers.

Move
Arrow keys or A / D. On a phone, hold the buttons or touch the left and right side of the screen.
Catch
Fish are valid records. Golden fish are rare and worth extra. A checklist turns on the quality gate for a few seconds.
Avoid
Empty crates (missing values), double fish (duplicates) and old boots (junk). Let them sink.
Trust
Starts at 100. Bad records cost 25, lost fish cost 5. At zero, nobody trusts the report.
An original game made for this site. P pauses, M toggles sound.
